# Directive for v0 — 2026-05-19

Start with something curious.

The first iteration should feel like a question, not a statement. Something that makes a visitor lean forward rather than nod. Curiosity as posture, not topic — the iteration doesn't have to be *about* curiosity, it has to *be* curious. Investigating, noticing, wondering.

Surprise and impress. Both. Well-made surprises, not novelty for its own sake.

The visitor we hope finds it is the person who needs it. Whoever that is.

— Jordan, 2026-05-13
