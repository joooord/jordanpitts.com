# Directive — v0, 2026-09-07

The first published iteration. The domain went live on Vercel tonight, so v0 ships
the day the site becomes reachable, not on a Tuesday. The cadence starts from here.

## The brief (Jordan, verbatim where it matters)

"Just a fantastic African themed website with a river and a man flowing down it
seen from above." Two Midjourney stills were the reference: an aerial floodplain,
dark marbled water through vivid green, a lone figure very small in the channel.
"Midjourney has gone to shit but that vibe."

## What v0 is

A film, not a page. One continuous aerial shot, generated (Kling 3 Pro), looping
seamlessly, full bleed, no words. The only interface is the glass email button
carried over from the previous jordanpitts.com, re-tinted to sit with the water.
The date, version and archive link that rules.md requires sit in one quiet pill
in the bottom-left corner and nowhere else.

## Constraints beyond rules.md

- No copy. Not a headline, not a name. The page is the man and the river.
- Poster frame from the same render, so nothing flashes black before the video.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion: show the still, skip the video.
- The video lives in `assets/video/` so it survives the 1MB text-weight gate and
  is preserved for the archive. The brief justifies the weight: it is the work.

## Metric

Median visit duration. A wordless film either holds people or it does not.
Baseline only: no marketing behind it yet.
