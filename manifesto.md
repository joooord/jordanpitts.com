# Manifesto

Direction for the current period. Updated by review. The generator reads this to set intent.

## The project, in one line
The best website for no reason — a place for learning and art(ish), regenerated weekly, archived in full.

## Current focus (week of 2026-05-13)

jordanpitts.com is an evolving website regenerated each Tuesday by Claude, archived in full, actively marketed. The site is the experiment. The goal is to **surprise and impress** — both, not either. Well-made surprises, not novelty for its own sake. No iteration is required to be about Jordan personally.

Early iterations favour range over coherence. Each Tuesday swings — different forms, eras, moods, structures, mediums. Coherence is required within a single iteration, not across them.

## Audience

The audience is **the person who needs it** — whoever that is, on any given Tuesday. Don't optimise for a demographic; optimise for resonance. The visitor who lands here and pauses, who comes back, who tells someone else — that is the audience. Their identity is not predetermined.

## What "good" looks like right now
- Surprising AND impressive — both, not either
- Specific over generic
- Confident over safe
- Coherent within an iteration, even if wildly different from the previous one
- Worth a return visit
- Worth telling someone else about

## In scope
- Multi-page sites, build steps, original assets, custom media
- JavaScript, animation, audio, interactivity — whatever the iteration calls for
- Acquiring real visitors through marketing (see `MARKETING.md`)
- A published timeline of iterations at `/timeline/` plus an RSS feed
- **Optional visitor questions per iteration** — when an iteration calls for it, the generator may include a question for visitors. **Rate-limited: at most one visitor question per two iterations.** Form-handling backend (Supabase) is to be wired up; for now, questions may be passive thought exercises or `mailto:` prompts. The generator is told whether the previous iteration had a question and forbidden from including one if so.
- **Splinter cells** — when an iteration resonates strongly, fork it into a new project of its own (see `SPLINTER.md`). The main site continues evolving; the splinter takes on its own life.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Fixed brand identity
- SEO as a primary optimisation target
- Persistent user accounts on the main site
