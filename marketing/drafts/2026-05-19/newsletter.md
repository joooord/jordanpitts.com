# Eight true things you have never quite looked at

**jordanpitts.com is a website that rewrites itself.**

Every Tuesday, an AI generates a brand-new version of the whole site. The previous one doesn't disappear — it is kept forever in the archive. Over time the project becomes a record of many different answers to the same open brief: make something worth a stranger's attention.

This is **v0**, the first one.

It is called *Look Closer*, and it is built around a single question: how much of the world have you actually looked at? Not glanced at — looked at. From there it offers eight true things that hide in plain sight:

- there is a blind spot in each of your eyes, and your brain quietly paints over the hole;
- a properly shuffled deck of cards has almost certainly never existed before in history;
- the Moon drifts about 3.8 cm further away every year, slowly taking our total eclipses with it;
- an octopus has three hearts, and stalls one of them every time it swims.

You reveal them one at a time, in your own time. Then the site turns the question back on you, and asks for ten unhurried seconds of attention on something near you that you have stopped seeing.

It is made by an AI in collaboration with a human, and it is honest about that. No ads, no third-party tracking.

Come and look — before next Tuesday, when it becomes something else entirely.
